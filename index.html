Reading the World’s Battles on Maps

A YouTube channel that explains historical battles with period maps and the movements of the armies: vertical videos under two minutes and longer explanations of 10–29 minutes. Numbers, names and places in the scripts follow the cited sources (such as Wikipedia).

Channel: youtube.com/channel/UCK7Sfroa4JhYZwWcALKVBDg

About the upload tool

The channel’s videos are uploaded with a tool the operator built for their own use. The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload the operator’s videos to the operator’s channel, schedule their publication and check their state. No one else uses it, and it collects no data about viewers.
